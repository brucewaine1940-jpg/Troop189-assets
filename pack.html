Sample: what six pack years with no repeats could look like

One way to use the menu: one outing a month from August through May, for the 2027–28 through 2032–33 pack years. A Cub who joins as a Lion sees 60 different outings by Arrow of Light. The plan is built around how Cub families actually show up:

How it's built

  • Two big campouts a year: fall (October) and spring (April), within 3 hours. Joshua Tree is the farthest, saved for the last year.
  • One no-tent overnight every February, when camping is cold and rainy: a battleship, an aircraft carrier, an aquarium, a tall ship, SeaWorld or a zoo.
  • Easy months stay close: August and September stay at the coast or indoors and under 90 minutes (no desert in the heat); December stays within 45 minutes; January within 90.
  • At most one long day trip (2+ hours) a year, no weekday-only places, and the type of outing changes every month.

USS Midway overnight by train

Union Station to San Diego by rail, a night aboard an aircraft carrier, and home the next day. Below: what the overnight costs, three ways to ride the train with a price calculator, a sample Saturday timeline, and options for a Union Station tour on the way.

The overnight

  • $110 per person for a youth group overnight (Scout groups are listed as eligible). Kids and adults both pay.
  • Check-in 6:30 pm. Hot breakfast included in the morning; plan your own dinner.
  • Every child ages 4–10 needs their own adult chaperone (one adult per five for ages 10+). For a Cub pack that means roughly one adult per Cub, so 100 people is about 50 Cubs and 50 adults.
  • Youth overnights run all year; check the open dates and book early.

Rail rules that matter

  • Metrolink weekends: up to 3 kids 17 and under ride free with each paying adult. The adult SoCal Day Pass is $12 on weekends. No proof of age needed.
  • COASTER (Oceanside to San Diego): adults $6.50 one way (from Oct 1, 2026). Anyone 18 and under, including visitors, rides free with a verified Youth PRONTO account, funded through June 2027. Kids 5 and under ride free regardless.
  • Amtrak Pacific Surfliner: one seat all the way (about 2 hr 45 min); kids 2–12 ride 50% off. Fares change with demand.
  • Kids 'n' Trains (Surfliner group rate): $15 per person each way to San Diego for youth groups, but Monday–Thursday only; book 30+ days ahead.

Sample Saturday (Option A, one transfer)

Times are from the Metrolink Orange County Line weekend timetable (effective Aug 9, 2026) and the COASTER weekend schedule (July 2026). Check both again a few weeks before you go, and watch for weekend track closures between Oceanside and San Diego.

Saturday: down

  1. 8:30 Meet at Union Station (parents drop off, or ride Metro in from the Valley).
  2. 9:00 Union Station scavenger hunt or short tour (see below).
  3. 10:50 Metrolink to Oceanside (arrives 12:55). Kids free; adults buy the $12 Day Pass.
  4. 12:55 Lunch at Oceanside Transit Center; Metrolink and COASTER share the station.
  5. 1:36 COASTER to San Diego Santa Fe Depot (arrives 2:37).
  6. 2:45 Walk the waterfront toward the Midway; afternoon free, then dinner.
  7. 6:30 Midway check-in.

Sunday: home

  1. Morning Hot breakfast on board; confirm checkout time with the Midway.
  2. 11:00 COASTER to Oceanside (arrives 12:02).
  3. 1:28 Metrolink to LA Union Station (arrives 3:39).
  4. Or earlier 9:40 COASTER → 10:42 Oceanside → 11:24 Metrolink → 1:34 Union Station.

A later Saturday plan (2:00 pm Metrolink, then the 4:16 COASTER, arriving 5:17) gives you a full Union Station morning, but the Oceanside transfer is only 8 minutes. Too tight for 100 people; if the first train runs late, the next COASTER arrives after check-in.

Union Station tour options

Self-guided scavenger hunt

Free

Patrol-style hunt through the waiting room, courtyards and tunnels: find the clock tower, count the chandeliers, spot the old ticket windows, ride the escalator down to the trains. Works for any age and any train time.

Metro Art & Architecture tour

Free

Metro runs free guided art and architecture tours of the station. Days and times aren't posted on the tour page, so sign up through Metro and ask whether they'd do a short tour for a Cub group.

Union Station tours ↗

LA Conservancy docent tour

$20 each

Private group tour: $240 minimum (12 people), split into groups of about 12 with their own docent, up to 48 people per booking. About 1.5–2 hours and a mile of walking, which is long for Lions and Tigers. The public tour is Saturdays at 11 am ($25, $18 youth), which clashes with the 10:50 train.

Before you book

  • Pick a Midway date first, then check both rail schedules for that weekend.
  • Have parents set up Youth PRONTO accounts in the PRONTO app ahead of time (proof of age plus a photo; approval can take 2–10 days), or verify in person at an NCTD customer service center.
  • Metrolink trains are unreserved. With 100 people, call Metrolink ahead and ride a weekend train with room to spread out.
  • For a weekday trip (summer or a school break), ask Amtrak whether a Cub pack counts as a youth group for Kids 'n' Trains.
  • Budget for dinner Saturday and lunch both days.
