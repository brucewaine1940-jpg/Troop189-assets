Home

  1. Your most recently logged activities, so you can see what's been going on at a glance.
  2. The awards you're currently closest to completing - a quick snapshot without digging through every tab.
  3. Heads up if you're turning 13.5 or 14 - a couple of awards have real deadlines tied to those ages, and Home is where that notice shows up.

Log

New Entry - Logging an Activity
  1. Choose what you did from the dropdown.
  2. Fill in whatever fields show up - hours, nights, miles, whatever applies to that activity.
  3. Tap Add Activity.
  4. Logging more than one thing? Repeat, then tap Log Activities once to save them all together.
My Log - Your History
  1. Everything you've logged, most recent first.
  2. Within 7 days of logging something, you can tap Edit or Delete right there to fix a mistake.
  3. Past that window, ask a leader - they can make changes on their end.

Progress

Checking Your Progress
  1. Active shows what you're currently working toward.
  2. Completed shows what you've already finished.
  3. Browse All Awards shows everything, even things you haven't started yet.
  4. Tap any card to see more detail about that specific award.

Checklist

  1. Every award, organized by family - tap a family name to expand it and see the individual requirements underneath.
  2. Tap the star on anything you want to focus on - that's what adds it to your Roadmap.
  3. Scroll to the bottom for links to other opportunities - Awards Central, scholarships, and a few more that aren't tracked directly in the app.

Roadmap

  1. Your own personal shortlist - only shows what you've starred on Checklist. Nothing starred yet? It'll look empty, and that's normal.
  2. Sorted closest-to-complete first, so it's an easy way to see what's worth finishing next.
  3. Some awards have real age cutoffs (PVSA, Congressional Award) - a countdown banner shows up here if one applies to you.
